A pet is a small cosmetic companion that sprints alongside you and shows up on the scoreboard.

Support Sprinto on Patreon or Ko-fi and while your support is active, you'll have a sprinting companion to join you on sprints.

## No loot boxes or "gacha".

Although your emoji pet begins with a random name and appearance, there's no reason to be anxious about receiving the exact pet you want. You can freely change the name and appearance of your pet in the `/pets` panel.

Pets from exclusive collections such as Pixel Pets can also be renamed and you can also freely change their appearance to any other pet from the same collection.

Some top supporters have received a Pixel Pet already but I haven't told them, so they'll only find out if they look in `/pets`, and then they'll probably be confused about what it is, and I still haven't posted how to get one anywhere or what they look like, which is why you're the first to know reading this documentation. ## If your subscription lapses

Since July 2026, pets remain in your collection even after your Patreon subscription lapses. They'll still be able to join sprints for a grace period of  to 2 weeks, but after that you may lose the sprinting "seat" in your `/pets` panel, meaning pets won't be able join sprints.

If you previously had a pet which has disappeared, Sprinto will attempt to reunite you with the same pet if you subscribe again on Patreon or Ko-fi.

The 🪑 seat button appears in `/pets` if you have an active subscription. It shows an expiry date if you're in the two-week grace period after a lapsed subscription, or if it's a temporary gift, or if you're on a free trial, or because I changed how it works and forgot to update this text.

Note (August 2026): Pets have changed a lot recently and there have been glitches. Please send a message on Patreon or Ko-fi if you have a problem with your pet.
