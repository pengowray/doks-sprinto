A pet is a small cosmetic companion that sprints alongside you and shows up on the scoreboard.

You don't have just one pet anymore, you have a collection.

{{<slash name="pets" >}}

Opens a panel where you can see everything you've got, pick which one sprints with you, rename it, change how it looks, or tuck it into an egg until you want it back.

The panel only opens from the slash command. {{<atsprintoembed "pets" >}} just points you back to it.

One button per pet, and the tick is on the one that's currently sprinting with you. Press a pet to open it. Nobody else in the channel sees the panel, with one exception: the `<3` button on a pet posts a message everyone can see.

Until you have a pet or a seat there's nothing to press. The panel tells you there's no sprint seat right now, so your companions are resting, and links back to this page.

The pet currently sprinting with you is your **companion**, and it occupies your **sprint seat**. You can only have one companion at a time, and you can't swap it out mid-sprint since the scoreboard's already running by then.

Supporting Sprinto is what gets you a seat, and the seat is held for two weeks after your support ends. Without one your pets stay in your collection but none of them sprint, and renaming or restyling waits until you have a seat again. The 🪑 seat button shows which seat you have and when it runs out.

## Getting pets

Pets aren't bought outright and they don't appear from nothing. They arrive by supporting Sprinto, or by carrying over from the old bot. A carried-over pet is already in your panel if you were supporting when Sprinto took over. If you weren't, it's out of sight until you support again, and then you get that same pet back.


Every pet you're given is yours to keep. Whether one can sprint depends on your sprint seat, not on the pet.
