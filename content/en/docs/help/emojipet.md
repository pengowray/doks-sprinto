A pet is a small cosmetic companion that sprints alongside you and shows up on the scoreboard.

You don't have just one pet anymore, you have a collection.

Opens a panel where you can see everything you've got, pick which one sprints with you, and rename it.

The pet currently sprinting with you is your **companion**, and it occupies your **sprint seat**. You can only have one companion at a time, and you can't swap it out mid-sprint since the scoreboard's already running by then.

## Getting pets

Pets aren't bought outright and they don't appear from nothing. They arrive by supporting Sprinto, by hitting certain milestones, or by being carried over if you already had one on the old bot.


Every pet in your collection is either **Adopted** (yours to keep) or **Visiting** (a guest passing through).
