## Pets

A pet is a small cosmetic companion that sprints alongside you and shows up on the scoreboard.

You don't have just one pet anymore, you have a collection.

{{<slash name="pets" >}}

Opens a panel where you can see everything you've got, pick which one sprints with you, rename it, or change how it looks.

The panel only opens from the slash command. {{<atsprintoembed "pets" >}} just points you back to it.

One button per pet. The tick is on the one that sprints with you, and a 🪶 joins it while a sprint is running. Press a pet to open it. The card shows when you adopted it, how many sprints it has joined, its lifetime words and its best single sprint. Renaming or restyling keeps those numbers, and a carried-over pet keeps its history from the old bot.

Nobody else in the channel sees the panel, and the `<3` button on a pet is private too: the thought bubble stays on the card. To pet your companion where the channel can see it, type it instead:

{{<atsprinto "pet pet" >}}

🐢 Nugget ｡oO(💓)
{{< /reply >}}

Until you have a pet or a seat there's nothing to press, just a link back to this page. With pets but no seat, the panel says they aren't joining sprints.

The pet that sprints with you is your **companion**, and it occupies your **sprint seat**. You can only have one companion at a time, and you can't swap it out mid-sprint since the scoreboard's already running by then.

Supporting Sprinto is what gets you a seat, and the seat is held for two weeks after your support ends. Without one your pets stay in your collection but none of them sprint, and renaming or restyling waits until you have a seat again.

The 🪑 seat button names the kind of seat you have. It shows a date only for the seats that run out on one: a free trial, which comes with a seat that ends with the trial, and a seat in those last two weeks.

## On the scoreboard

Your companion gets a line of its own, ranked among the writers.

{{< reply >}}
🏆 **CONGRATS EVERYONE**
`1.` {{< mention "alex" >}} — **500 words** (33 wpm)
`2.` 🐢 Nugget — **300 words**
`3.` {{< mention "jo" >}} — **120 words** (8 wpm)
{{< /reply >}}

The count is cosmetic: a fraction of yours, rolled fresh each sprint, and left out of the room's combined total. If you deleted more than you wrote, your companion joins you in the deletions below the ranking.

## Getting pets

Pets aren't bought outright and they don't appear from nothing. They arrive by supporting Sprinto, or by carrying over from the old bot. A carried-over pet is already in your panel if you were supporting when Sprinto took over. If you weren't, it's out of sight until you support again, and then you get that same pet back.


Every pet you're given is yours to keep. Whether one can sprint depends on your sprint seat, not on the pet.
