Sprinting with Sprinto is free. Supporting Sprinto on [Patreon](https://patreon.com/pengowray) or [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/pengowray) gives you a **companion seat**: one of your pets joins your sprints and appears on the scoreboard.

## What supporters get

- **One companion seat.** The pet in the seat is your companion. Your companion joins every sprint you join and gets a line of their own on the scoreboard.
- You choose your companion in {{<slashembed name="pets">}}. You can change your companion at any time, except during a sprint you're in.
- If you have no pets when your seat starts, Sprinto gives you a first companion with a random look.

For more about pets, see [Pets]({{<relref "emojipet" >}}).

## How long your seat lasts

### Patreon

While your Patreon pledge is active, you keep your companion seat. Every Patreon tier gets the same companion seat.

### Ko-fi

A Ko-fi payment gives you a companion seat for a number of days that depends on the amount. A Ko-fi monthly membership keeps your seat while you're supporting.

## Check your seat

Press the **companion seat** button in {{<slashembed name="pets">}} to see which pet is in your seat and what kind of seat you have. The lines it shows are listed on the Pets page, under [Your seat's details]({{<relref "emojipet" >}}#your-seats-details).

In the last 7 days of your seat, the /pets panel also shows the date your seat ends: "Your companion seat lasts until 12 November."

## When your seat runs out

- Your pets stay in your collection.
- Your companion stays chosen, but stops joining sprints.
- Renaming a pet or changing their look needs a seat. You can still choose your companion, and hibernate or revive pets.
- If you support Sprinto again, your seat comes back and the same companion sprints with you again.

If you try to rename a pet or change their look without a seat, Sprinto replies:

{{< reply ephemeral="1" >}}
Your pets are resting. A companion seat lets one sit with you and appear on the scoreboard. You can still hibernate or revive them.
{{< /reply >}}
