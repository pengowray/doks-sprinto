Help keep Sprinto alive and support continued development on [Patreon](https://patreon.com/pengowray) & [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/pengowray). Thank you!!

## Linking your support

On Patreon, connect your Discord account to your Patreon account and Sprinto finds you on its own. Your sprint seat and pet usually turn up within a minute, and by the next day at the latest. If a day goes by and nothing has arrived, say so with {{<slashembed name="feedback" >}}.

On Ko-fi, or on Patreon with no Discord account connected, you'll be sent a code instead. Use it in Discord:

{{<slash name="redeem" key0="code" val0="_your code_">}}

That connects your support to your Discord account. If you use the same email address on Ko-fi as on Patreon and Patreon is already linked, your Ko-fi support finds the same Discord account on its own.

The same command claims a gift code someone has sent you. A gift is either a companion or months of supporter status, and Sprinto says which when you redeem it. A gift companion waits in your {{<slashembed name="pets" >}} panel, named Gift companion until you rename it, and brings a sprint seat that lasts as long as the gift.

Only you see the answer, and it works in a DM with Sprinto as well as in a server.

If Sprinto says that support is already linked to another Discord account, the code was used from a different one. Ask with {{<slashembed name="feedback" >}} and it can be moved over.

## One-off tips

A one-off tip on Ko-fi gets you a sprint seat, without a monthly pledge. How long it lasts depends on the size: 44 days for a small tip, 60 days from $10, 90 days from $20, and a year from $50, counted from the day you tip.

The seat arrives once your Ko-fi account is linked, but the clock runs from the day you tipped, so link it first if you can.
