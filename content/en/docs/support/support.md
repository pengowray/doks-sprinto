Help keep Sprinto alive and support continued development on [Patreon](https://patreon.com/pengowray) & [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/pengowray). Thank you!!

## Linking your support

On Patreon, connect your Discord account to your Patreon account and Sprinto finds you on its own. Your sprint seat and pet usually turn up within a minute, and by the next day at the latest.

On Ko-fi, or on Patreon with no Discord account connected, you'll be sent a code instead. Use it in Discord:

{{<slash name="redeem" key0="code" val0="_your code_">}}

That connects your support to your Discord account. The same command claims a gift code someone has sent you; a gift pet then waits for you in your {{<slashembed name="pets" >}} panel.

Only you see the answer, and it works in a DM with Sprinto as well as in a server.
